If you are tired of being part of the modern world with its constant hussle,
then join the ~dark side~ role-playing community and be who you want to be!

On a cloudy March day, inside Messe Kassel there were crowds of people in shiny medieval (and not only medieval) outfits.

LARPCon is a place to show off your existing costumes and buy props for the next one.
And we were able to do both, and also take a ton of cool pictures.
